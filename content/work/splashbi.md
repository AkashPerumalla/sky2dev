Worked on query-based enterprise reporting over Oracle EBS Procure-to-Pay and
Order-to-Cash data, writing SQL and PL/SQL against schemas that had accumulated
a decade of exceptions.

The substantial piece was a migration: 25–50 legacy Discoverer reports moved
through EIS Explorer onto the SplashBI platform. The hard part wasn't the
porting — it was proving the numbers still matched. Each report had to be
validated across all three systems, because a reporting migration that silently
changes a total is worse than one that fails outright.

Also built RESTful APIs in Node.js for blog management workflows, covering CRUD
operations and backend service integration.

## What I took from it

Enterprise data is where you learn that correctness is a social problem as much
as a technical one. The schema tells you what's allowed; only the people using
it can tell you what the numbers are supposed to mean.
